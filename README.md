Introduction

Mars Petcare’s supply chain supports the delivery of pet nutrition, care, and health products to pets and pet owners worldwide. With a presence in more than 130 countries and approximately 100,000 Petcare Associates, its global operations encompass nutrition, veterinary health, diagnostics, science, innovation, and technology. [1] Coordinating these activities is essential to supporting product availability, quality, and the company’s purpose of creating A BETTER WORLD FOR PETS. [1]

2.1 Company Profile

Mars, Incorporated is a family-owned global business that has developed over more than a century. Founded in 1911 by Frank C. Mars, the company began with the production and sale of butter cream candy from his kitchen in Tacoma, Washington, USA. Over the years, Mars has evolved into a diversified business with a portfolio of confectionery, food, and pet care products and services, operating across international markets. Headquartered in McLean, Virginia, USA, Mars describes itself as family-owned, future-facing, and purpose-driven, with its business guided by its Purpose and Five Principles: Quality, Responsibility, Mutuality, Efficiency, and Freedom.
