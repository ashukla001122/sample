Introduction

Mars Petcare’s supply chain supports the delivery of pet nutrition, care, and health products to pets and pet owners worldwide. With a presence in more than 130 countries and approximately 100,000 Petcare Associates, its global operations encompass nutrition, veterinary health, diagnostics, science, innovation, and technology. [1] Coordinating these activities is essential to supporting product availability, quality, and the company’s purpose of creating A BETTER WORLD FOR PETS. [1]

2.1 Company Profile

Mars, Incorporated is a family-owned global business that has developed over more than a century. Founded in 1911 by Frank C. Mars, the company began with the production and sale of butter cream candy from his kitchen in Tacoma, Washington, USA. Over the years, Mars has evolved into a diversified business with a portfolio of confectionery, food, and pet care products and services, operating across international markets. Headquartered in McLean, Virginia, USA, Mars describes itself as family-owned, future-facing, and purpose-driven, with its business guided by its Purpose and Five Principles: Quality, Responsibility, Mutuality, Efficiency, and Freedom. [2][3]





Mars, Incorporated has evolved from a confectionery business founded in 1911 into a global company with a diversified portfolio of products and services. Its expansion into Petcare, investments in pet nutrition research, and strategic acquisitions have played a significant role in shaping the development of Mars Petcare. [3]


1. 1911 — Foundation of Mars
Frank C. Mars began making and selling butter cream candy from his kitchen in Tacoma, Washington, USA.
2. 1935 — Entry into Petcare
Mars entered the Petcare business through the acquisition of Chappel Brothers, Ltd., maker of CHAPPIE dog food.
3. 1965 — Pet Nutrition Research
The Waltham Centre for Pet Nutrition was established in the UK.
4. 1968 — Expansion into the US Petcare Market
Mars acquired Kal Kan Foods, expanding its presence in the US pet food market.
5. 2002 — Royal Canin Acquisition
Royal Canin became part of Mars Petcare, strengthening its specialized pet nutrition portfolio.
6. 2015 — Mars Petcare Expansion
Mars further expanded its Petcare business, strengthening its position across pet nutrition and veterinary healthcare.
